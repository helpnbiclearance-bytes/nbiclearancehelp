How Long Does NBI Clearance Take?

There is no single accurate time for every application. No-HIT branch processing can proceed quickly after biometrics, HIT review takes longer, mailed clearance has its own timeline, and eGovPH renewal may be much faster for eligible users.

Why “same day” can be misleading

A no-HIT application may proceed to printing and release after biometric capture and record checking, but your total visit time also depends on queue length, office conditions and whether your transaction is ready. A Citizen’s Charter duration for an individual service step is not the same thing as a guaranteed door-to-door appointment duration.

HIT applications

A HIT creates a separate manual-review timeline. The current NBI how-to page describes 5 to 10 working days as the usual return period, while the exact branch instruction should control your case.

Applications from abroad

NBI’s mailed-clearance page says processing takes a maximum of five working days after the documents are received. Courier or international-mail transit is outside that processing figure.

Processing-time FAQs

Can I get NBI Clearance in one day?

A no-HIT branch application can move to printing during the same visit after biometric and record checks, but queue and office conditions vary.

Does a HIT always take 10 days?

No. NBI describes a usual 5–10 working-day period; follow the exact return date from your branch.

How fast is eGovPH renewal?

NBI says eligible users may renew in as little as five minutes through eGovPH.
