Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for NBI Clearance Help, including a strict policy against collecting NBI passwords, OTPs or ID scans.

Our privacy approach is data-minimizing by design.

Sensitive NBI information

We do not need and do not ask users to submit NBI passwords, OTPs, full government-ID scans, complete NBI reference numbers or account credentials.

Analytics, advertising and cookies

We may use privacy-conscious analytics to understand aggregate site usage. If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to serve, measure and personalize ads where permitted.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies in your browser as a result of ads shown on this website. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services and manage ad personalization in your Google account.

Where applicable law requires consent, an appropriate consent message will be shown before non-essential advertising or analytics cookies are used.

External links

The site links to official NBI and government websites. Those destinations have their own privacy practices and are outside our control.
