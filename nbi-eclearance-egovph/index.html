NBI eClearance on the eGovPH Super App

NBI and DICT launched eClearance on eGovPH on 6 July 2026. Eligible verified users can access NBI Clearance services through the app, including renewal and digital-copy capabilities.

Official NBI Portal ↗

What NBI officially announced

The official NBI launch statement says verified eGovPH users can access NBI Clearance services through a secure interoperable platform. It specifically mentions the ability to retrieve, renew and access a digital copy of NBI Clearance.

How fast is renewal?

NBI says eligible users may renew in as little as five minutes through the app. That is an official launch claim, not a promise that every user or every record will complete in five minutes.

What we do not assume

The NBI launch announcement does not document every app-screen edge case, fee combination or error message. Our site labels those details as app observations unless they are explicitly documented by NBI or DICT.

Why eGovPH matters for NBI Clearance

The eGovPH integration is a major change because it moves part of the NBI Clearance experience into a broader verified government-service app. NBI describes the integration as secure and interoperable and says it reduces the need for long lines for eligible renewal users.

Renewal

The launch announcement says eligible users may renew in as little as five minutes. That does not mean a new applicant can automatically skip every normal identity or branch requirement.

Retrieval and digital copy

NBI says verified users can retrieve services and access a digital copy. Where a receiving employer or institution needs a particular form of clearance, confirm what that institution accepts.

Check eligibility inside your verified account

The NBI announcement confirms retrieval, renewal and digital-copy capabilities for verified eGovPH users. It does not say that every person or every old clearance receives identical options. Treat the choices shown inside your verified account as the current eligibility result.

Account and phone safety

Install eGovPH only through an official app-store listing linked by government channels. Keep control of the mobile number used for verification and never share an OTP. App screens can change, so this guide focuses on decisions and safety checks rather than claiming every button label is permanent.

eGovPH FAQs

When did NBI eClearance launch on eGovPH?

The NBI and DICT formally launched it on 6 July 2026.

Can every user renew in five minutes?

No. NBI says eligible users may renew in as little as five minutes; eligibility and record conditions still matter.

Is the digital copy officially supported?

Yes. The NBI launch announcement explicitly mentions access to a digital copy for verified users.
