About NBI Clearance Help

About the independent NBI Clearance Help website, its Philippines focus and what the site does and does not do.

Official NBI Portal ↗

NBI Clearance Help is an independent informational website focused on making NBI Clearance procedures easier to understand for people in the Philippines.

What we do

We research official NBI sources, organize the application and renewal process into clear guides, track meaningful process changes and explain common problems such as HIT status, appointment timing and payment issues.

What we do not do

  • We do not issue NBI Clearances.
  • We do not process NBI payments.
  • We do not book appointments on behalf of users.
  • We are not affiliated with the National Bureau of Investigation, Department of Justice or Philippine Government.
  • We do not ask users for passwords, OTPs or identity-document scans.

How our guides are prepared

Each guide is written for a specific user task and checked against primary sources such as NBI.gov.ph, the official NBI Clearance portal and applicable Philippine government publications. We show a verification date, link to the key sources and clearly label illustrations that are not official portal screenshots.

Editorial responsibility

NBI Clearance Help is responsible for the content published on this website. We review material when official processes change and maintain dedicated editorial, source and corrections pages so readers can understand how information was prepared and how to report an error.

Country focus
