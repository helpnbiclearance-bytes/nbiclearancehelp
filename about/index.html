About NBI Clearance Help

About the independent NBI Clearance Help website, its Philippines focus and what the site does and does not do.

Official NBI Portal ↗

NBI Clearance Help is an independently published information website for people navigating NBI Clearance procedures in the Philippines and for Filipinos applying from abroad. Our purpose is to turn official instructions into practical, easy-to-follow guidance while keeping the official transaction channels unmistakably separate from this website.

What we publish

We publish task-focused guides covering online registration, appointments, requirements, valid IDs, fees, payments, HIT status, renewal routes, first-time job seekers, eGovPH services, branches and applications from abroad. Each page is intended to answer a real applicant question and lead readers to the appropriate official source for any transaction.

What we do not do

  • We do not issue, renew, authenticate or verify NBI Clearances.
  • We do not accept payments, reserve appointments or access NBI accounts.
  • We do not represent the National Bureau of Investigation, Department of Justice, DICT, eGovPH or any Philippine government agency.
  • We do not offer a fixer, priority lane or guaranteed outcome.
  • We do not ask for passwords, OTPs, full ID images or complete reference numbers.

How our guides are prepared

We begin with primary material such as NBI.gov.ph pages, the official NBI Clearance portal, Citizen’s Charter documents and relevant Philippine government announcements. We compare the source instructions, identify details that can affect an applicant’s next step and explain uncertainties instead of presenting assumptions as official rules.

Illustrations on this website are original explanatory graphics. Unless a caption expressly says otherwise, they are not screenshots of the official portal and should not be used to predict the exact appearance of a live government system.

Who is responsible for the website

NBI Clearance Help is responsible for the selection, organization and presentation of its independently published content. Editorial questions, factual corrections and website feedback can be sent to helpnbiclearance@gmail.com.

Our standard for useful content

We aim to publish information that helps a reader complete a specific task safely. We avoid copied instructions, invented timelines, guaranteed results and pages created only to repeat a search phrase. When a fee, branch detail or portal feature can change, we direct readers to the live official source for confirmation.
