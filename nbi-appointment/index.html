NBI Appointment Online: Booking, Schedule & Branch Visit

Use the official NBI Clearance portal to choose a branch and available AM or PM schedule. This guide also covers no-slot situations, missed dates and walk-in confusion.

By NBI Clearance Help Editorial TeamUpdated 11 October 2026
Official NBI Portal ↗

How to Book an NBI Appointment

  1. Open the official NBI Clearance portal.
  2. Complete or review your applicant profile.
  3. Start the NBI Clearance application.
  4. Choose an available branch and schedule.
  5. Complete payment through an official supported channel.
  6. Save your reference number, selected branch and date, and proof of payment.

Booking is part of the larger application flow, so if you have not registered yet, start with the complete application guide. Before you choose a date, confirm you meet the NBI Clearance requirements — an appointment is useless if your IDs will not pass the branch check.

If there are no available slots

Do not rely on claims that slots always appear at a particular hour unless the NBI itself publishes that rule. Check other available dates or branches in the live portal, then use the official office locator to confirm location information before traveling. The dedicated no-slots guide walks through every safe option in detail.

Avoid fixers and slot sellers. Appointment availability is controlled by the official NBI system. Do not send money to a person who promises a hidden slot, fast lane or guaranteed clearance.

Understanding AM and PM schedules

The portal generally offers morning (AM) and afternoon (PM) schedule windows rather than exact clock times. Your choice should be driven by travel reality, not by which window looks less busy on screen.

Pick the window you can actually attend

A morning slot means leaving home early enough to arrive comfortably before the window, accounting for traffic. An afternoon slot gives you more travel flexibility but can run into end-of-day queues. Choose the window where a 30-minute delay still leaves you safe.

The schedule is a window, not a queue number

Your AM or PM selection tells the branch which half of the day to expect you; it does not guarantee you will be processed at a specific minute. Arriving early inside your window is still the best way to keep the visit short.

Choosing the right NBI branch and schedule

The best NBI appointment is not necessarily the first slot you see. Choose an office you can realistically reach and a date that gives you enough time to prepare the required IDs and payment proof. If the portal offers AM and PM windows, select the one you can attend without depending on an extremely tight travel schedule.

Check the guide to finding and verifying an NBI Clearance branch before confirming your appointment.

Before confirming the branch

  • Check the official NBI office locator for the current location.
  • Make sure you are choosing the branch you actually intend to visit.
  • Review your profile and transaction before paying.
  • Save the branch name, appointment date and reference number after the transaction is created.

The official locator is more reliable than a third-party list copied months earlier because branches and service arrangements can change.

Do not book a branch you cannot reach twice

A HIT means returning on a later date, usually after 5 to 10 working days. If you choose a far-away branch for a slightly earlier slot, a HIT turns that small saving into two long trips. A nearer branch with a slightly later first date is often the better overall choice.

No NBI appointment slots: what should you do?

A “no slots” situation is a booking problem, not a reason to use a fixer. Start by checking the live official portal and other practical branch choices. If your preferred location is full, compare nearby official offices using the NBI locator. Avoid articles that promise a secret hour when slots “always” open unless NBI itself publishes such a schedule.

Safe next steps

  1. Check other dates in the same branch.
  2. Check another realistic branch in the official system.
  3. Confirm that you are on the correct transaction and applicant route.
  4. Return to the live portal later if your timeline allows.
  5. Do not send money to a person selling a slot.

For the full playbook, work through the safe next steps above and return to the live portal later if your timeline allows.

Patience beats payment. Slots open and close as other applicants book, cancel or reschedule. Checking back over several days is free and legitimate; paying a stranger for a “reserved” slot is neither.

Appointment confirmation: what to save

After payment, your appointment exists as data in the NBI system — but you should keep your own copy of every detail. If anything goes wrong at the branch, these are what you will be asked for.

Save this Why it matters
Reference number Links your payment and branch visit to the online application
Branch name Confirms you travel to the office tied to your transaction
Schedule date and window (AM/PM) Defines your target visit; relevant to the 15-day grace period
Payment proof or receipt Backup if the payment status needs verification

Screenshot everything and keep the images in more than one place — your phone gallery and an email to yourself is a simple, reliable backup. A printed copy is a sensible extra precaution, not a requirement.

What if you miss the appointment date?

The current NBI how-to guide states that recent system updates allow a 15-day grace period once the transaction is marked PAID, meaning applicants can typically visit the chosen branch within that period if they miss the exact date. Because system rules and branch implementation can change, verify your transaction status before travel.

Missed vs rescheduled: what is the difference?

Missing the date means simply not showing up; the grace period is what protects your paid transaction afterward. Rescheduling — where the portal offers it — means actively moving your target date before it passes. If the portal shows a reschedule option for your transaction, using it is cleaner than relying on the grace period. The missed appointment guide covers both situations step by step.

Do not assume the grace period is unlimited. It is described as a 15-day window tied to a paid transaction. If you are approaching the end of that window, prioritize the branch visit and confirm the transaction status first.

Can you walk in without an appointment?

Official NBI pages are not fully synchronized. The current How to Apply page emphasizes the online application and scheduling process, while an older Citizen’s Charter still describes walk-in applicants. The official office locator tells applicants to apply and pay online before going to a clearance office.

Our publishing rule. We do not say “walk-ins are always allowed” or “walk-ins are banned nationwide.” Follow the live portal and confirm with your selected branch if your situation does not fit the normal online flow.

When to call the branch first

If you cannot complete the online flow — for example, a profile problem blocks scheduling — contact the branch or NBI help channels before travelling. Showing up unannounced with an incomplete transaction is the scenario most likely to end in a wasted trip.

Renewal vs new application: does the appointment differ?

It depends on whether your renewal qualifies for a branch-free route. Eligible renewals through eGovPH eClearance or the online renewal option for post-2014 clearances with unchanged details can avoid a branch visit entirely. If your renewal does not qualify — or your details changed — the appointment flow works the same as a new application: branch, schedule, payment and visit.

The NBI Clearance renewal guide explains which route fits your previous certificate, and the fee guide covers what you will pay.

Program-specific documents Needed for routes such as First-Time Job Seeker or mailed clearance

For the reasoning behind each item — and the expiry-date trap that ruins branch visits — read what to bring to your NBI appointment in full.

Appointment-day preparation

Bring the same identity documents that support the information in your online profile. Current NBI guidance says standard applicants should have their reference number or QR code, proof of payment and two original, unexpired government-issued IDs. Arriving without a required ID can turn a simple branch visit into a wasted trip.

Dress and photo considerations

The current NBI how-to page advises applicants to avoid sandos, tank tops, shorts and slippers and recommends smart casual or a collared shirt for the official photo. A branch can enforce practical appearance rules for photo capture, so dress conservatively if you are unsure.

The night-before routine

Pack your bag the evening before: both IDs, the reference number (on your phone and printed), and payment proof. Check traffic and the branch location. Applicants who prepare in the morning are the ones who discover a missing document halfway there.

At the branch

Follow the branch queue and staff instructions. Keep your phone accessible in case staff need to see the reference number or QR code. If anything about your transaction looks wrong — wrong branch, wrong name spelling — raise it with staff before biometrics, not after.

NBI appointment FAQs

How do I book an NBI appointment online?

Register or log in on the official NBI Clearance portal, complete your profile, click Apply for Clearance, choose an available branch and AM or PM schedule, pay through an official channel, then save your reference number.

Can I choose any NBI branch for my appointment?

Choose from the branch options available for your official transaction and schedule. Confirm the selected office before paying; verify the location through the official NBI office locator.

Can I change my appointment date or branch after paying?

Rescheduling options are controlled by the live portal and can change. Check your transaction for the current choices. A paid transaction typically carries a 15-day grace period, which gives flexibility if you miss the exact date.

What if I paid but missed my target date?

The current NBI how-to page says a paid transaction typically has a 15-day grace period after the target window. Check the live transaction and branch instructions because system rules can change.

Are walk-ins allowed?

Official pages are not perfectly synchronized. The current flow emphasizes online application and payment, while an older Citizen’s Charter describes walk-in processing. Use the live portal and confirm with the branch.

How early should I arrive at the branch?

Plan to arrive comfortably before your schedule window. Branches can be busy, and arriving early gives you buffer for queues, document checks and any unexpected issue with your transaction.

Do I need to print my appointment confirmation?

The reference number or QR code is the key identifier. A saved screenshot on your phone usually works, but carrying a printed copy as backup is a sensible precaution.

Can someone sell me an earlier appointment?

Avoid unofficial slot sellers. Use appointment options created by the official NBI system.

What should I do if there are no available slots?

Check other dates in the same branch, try another realistic branch in the official system, and return to the live portal later. Do not pay anyone who promises a hidden slot.
