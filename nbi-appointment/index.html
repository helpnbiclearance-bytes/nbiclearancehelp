NBI Appointment Online: Booking, Schedule & Branch Visit

Use the official NBI Clearance portal to choose a branch and available AM or PM schedule. This guide also covers no-slot situations, missed dates and walk-in confusion.

Official NBI Portal ↗

How to Book an NBI Appointment

  1. Open the official NBI Clearance portal.
  2. Complete or review your applicant profile.
  3. Start the NBI Clearance application.
  4. Choose an available branch and schedule.
  5. Complete payment through an official supported channel.
  6. Save your reference number, selected branch and date, and proof of payment.

If there are no available slots

Do not rely on claims that slots always appear at a particular hour unless the NBI itself publishes that rule. Check other available dates or branches in the live portal, then use the official office locator to confirm location information before traveling.

Avoid fixers and slot sellers. Appointment availability is controlled by the official NBI system. Do not send money to a person who promises a hidden slot, fast lane or guaranteed clearance.

What if you miss the appointment date?

The current NBI how-to guide states that recent system updates allow a 15-day grace period once the transaction is marked PAID, meaning applicants can typically visit the chosen branch within that period if they miss the exact date. Because system rules and branch implementation can change, verify your transaction status before travel.

Can you walk in without an appointment?

Official NBI pages are not fully synchronized. The current How to Apply page emphasizes the online application and scheduling process, while an older Citizen’s Charter still describes walk-in applicants. The official office locator tells applicants to apply and pay online before going to a clearance office.

Our publishing rule. We do not say “walk-ins are always allowed” or “walk-ins are banned nationwide.” Follow the live portal and confirm with your selected branch if your situation does not fit the normal online flow.

What to bring to the appointment

Item Why it matters
Reference number / QR code Links your branch visit to the online transaction
Proof of payment Useful if the payment status needs to be checked
Two original, unexpired government IDs Current NBI guidance for the standard application
Program-specific documents Needed for routes such as First-Time Job Seeker or mailed clearance

Choosing the right NBI branch and schedule

The best NBI appointment is not necessarily the first slot you see. Choose an office you can realistically reach and a date that gives you enough time to prepare the required IDs and payment proof. If the portal offers AM and PM windows, select the one you can attend without depending on an extremely tight travel schedule.

Check the guide to finding and verifying an NBI Clearance branch before confirming your appointment.

Before confirming the branch

  • Check the official NBI office locator for the current location.
  • Make sure you are choosing the branch you actually intend to visit.
  • Review your profile and transaction before paying.
  • Save the branch name, appointment date and reference number after the transaction is created.

The official locator is more reliable than a third-party list copied months earlier because branches and service arrangements can change.

No NBI appointment slots: what should you do?

A “no slots” situation is a booking problem, not a reason to use a fixer. Start by checking the live official portal and other practical branch choices. If your preferred location is full, compare nearby official offices using the NBI locator. Avoid articles that promise a secret hour when slots “always” open unless NBI itself publishes such a schedule.

Safe next steps

  1. Check other dates in the same branch.
  2. Check another realistic branch in the official system.
  3. Confirm that you are on the correct transaction and applicant route.
  4. Return to the live portal later if your timeline allows.
  5. Do not send money to a person selling a slot.

Appointment-day preparation

Bring the same identity documents that support the information in your online profile. Current NBI guidance says standard applicants should have their reference number or QR code, proof of payment and two original, unexpired government-issued IDs. Arriving without a required ID can turn a simple branch visit into a wasted trip.

Dress and photo considerations

The current NBI how-to page advises applicants to avoid sandos, tank tops, shorts and slippers and recommends smart casual or a collared shirt for the official photo. A branch can enforce practical appearance rules for photo capture, so dress conservatively if you are unsure.

NBI appointment FAQs

Can I choose any NBI branch for my appointment?

Choose from the branch options available for your official transaction and schedule. Confirm the selected office before paying; the NBI Clearance branches guide explains how to verify a practical location.

What if I paid but missed my target date?

The current NBI how-to page says a paid transaction typically has a 15-day grace period after the target window. Check the live transaction and branch instructions because system rules can change.

Are walk-ins allowed?

Official pages are not perfectly synchronized. The current flow emphasizes online application and payment, while an older Citizen’s Charter describes walk-in processing. Use the live portal and confirm with the branch.

Can someone sell me an earlier appointment?

Avoid unofficial slot sellers. Use appointment options created by the official NBI system.
