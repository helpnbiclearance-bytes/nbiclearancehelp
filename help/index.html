How to use this Help Center safely

Choose the problem category, follow the public troubleshooting steps, and move to official support only when an account-specific intervention is required. This avoids sharing sensitive information with a third-party guide.

Account problem

Use the official login and recovery path. Do not send passwords or OTPs to anyone.

Payment problem

Keep the receipt and reference number, verify the original transaction and avoid duplicate payment.

HIT problem

Follow the branch’s return date and any Quality Control or document instructions.

Appointment problem

Use the live portal and official locator. Do not buy slots from unofficial sellers.
