How to Get NBI Clearance While Abroad

NBI has a separate mailed-clearance process for applicants outside the Philippines. New applications and renewals abroad should follow that official route instead of a normal local appointment guide.

Official NBI Portal ↗

Official overseas process

The NBI mailed-clearance page explains procedures for documents prepared abroad and for applications handled by a designated representative in the Philippines.

Designated representative route

Before going to the NBI Mailed Clearance Section, the representative must register the applicant online at the official NBI portal, choose NBI Main Clearance Center as the preferred site, select a payment channel, secure the reference number and complete payment.

Processing and renewal notes

  • NBI states a maximum of five working days upon receipt of documents.
  • Applications from abroad are processed at the NBI Clearance Building on U.N. Avenue, Ermita, Manila.
  • Clearance certificates issued starting 2014 may be renewed through mail or a designated representative using the mailed-clearance procedures.
  • Clearances issued before 2014 are treated as new applications for this purpose.

New application abroad vs renewal abroad

These are not identical. A new overseas application follows the mailed-clearance requirements for identity documents and fingerprints. A renewal can use the mailed or designated-representative procedure only when the existing clearance meets the NBI’s date rule.

Clearances issued before 2014

The NBI mailed-clearance page says they are treated as new applications for this process rather than renewals.

Clearances issued starting 2014

They may be renewed by mail or designated representative using the published mailed-clearance procedures.

After you receive the clearance abroad

NBI instructs recipients to examine the certificate for the embossed NBI dry seal and affix the right thumbmark in the space provided. Follow the latest official instructions included with your processed certificate.

Plan the overseas application as a document chain

An application from abroad involves identity and fingerprint preparation, delivery to the Philippines, processing at the designated NBI office and return delivery or representative collection. The NBI’s handling time does not include every international transit and preparation step, so allow time for each handoff.

Confirm the receiving organization’s rules

The organization requesting the clearance decides how recent it must be and whether authentication, translation or legalization is also required. Check those rules before starting so mailing time does not leave you with a document that is unsuitable for its intended use.

Applying from abroad FAQs

Where are overseas applications processed?

NBI says all clearance applications coming from abroad are processed at the NBI Clearance Building on United Nations Avenue, Ermita, Manila.

How long does mailed clearance take?

NBI states a maximum processing time of five working days upon receipt of documents. Mailing/transit time is separate.

Can a representative handle the Philippine side?

Yes. NBI publishes a designated-representative procedure with online registration, payment and presentation of documents at the Mailed Clearance Section.
