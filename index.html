Why NBI Clearance Needed

This Online Portal is an impactful step by the Philippine Government to cope with increasing crimes, drugs, and fraudulent activities. Of course, the process helps authorities to track and hunt individuals with criminal records.

Legitimate Document

The NBI Online Application Form is a legitimate document issued by the National Bureau of Investigation under the Department of Justice. Notably, the NBI Database verifies each NBI appointment application before approval.

National Coverage

NBI Record Check is not limited to the NBI. In practice, all Clearance Applications cross-match the databases of all major law enforcement agencies, including the Philippine National Police, the Jail Management, and the Drug Enforcement.

Digital Record System

It’s impossible to bypass or forge the NBI Appointment Registration, as the entire system is digitized. NBI authorities store the applicant’s private details, biometrics, and photos in their end-to-end encrypted database.

Official Validity Period

This Online Application Form has a 1-year expiration date. Eventually, applicants need a new Appointment for NBI Renewal, and this method helps NBI authorities access up-to-date records and statuses for individuals.

Unique Tracking IDs

NBI assigns a unique ID number for each application. In the meantime, applicants can access the official website, provide their tracking IDs, and check their current NBI status without hassle.

What is Purpose of NBI Clearance Philippines

This Appointment new is mandatory for local and overseas individuals for all legitimate reasons. Indeed, the Philippine Government has the authority to deny or seize several Civil Rights on the grounds of NBI Clearance.

1🔍

Record Verification

The foremost reason to Apply For this process online is to verify an individual’s criminal record through the Government database. Of course, the portal is among the notable initiatives by the Philippine Government to maintain law and order.

2💼

Employment

All citizens should submit the Application for employment purposes in the Philippines or Overseas. In practice, this process ensures job access to applicants with spotless criminal records.

3📄

Transactions

Before engaging in Government-based transactions for civil service exams, visa applications, and work permits, applicants should set an NBI Appointment Schedule. Otherwise, the concerned departments can reject your applications.

4✈️

Travelers

Travelers, tourists, and foreigners in the Philippines should schedule their Appointment Online Schedule before interacting with the Immigration Department. The Government may deny Visas, Study Plans, or Business Ventures for foreigners without this Clearance.

5🏢

Business

Before applying for Business Permits, Firearms Licenses, Driver’s Licenses, or other Legal Activities, applicants should visit the NBI Online Website or a physical branch and claim their NBI Clearance Certificates.

Verify NBI Authenticity

Existing NBI clearance holders can also check the authenticity and remaining validity of their certificates. For online Verification, applicants should visit “https://verification.nbi-clearance.io/” and provide their ID Number to check the results.

The ID Number is an 18-digit unique code printed in the upper-left or upper-right section of your Clearance certificate. Meanwhile, the Verification Portal also integrates a “Scanning Option,” but it requires a working Camera on your device.

How to Apply For NBI Online (Step-by-Step Guide)

NBI offers two channels for this process, including the online Application and NBI Field Offices. In all seriousness, the NBI online portal is convenient and streamlined, but applicants without the internet can apply for manually.

These are few of the most effective and less time taking mediums in order to get NBI Philippines instantly. If you want to keep your private information secured and confidential, then the below mediums can be trusted with closed eyes.

Visit Official Portal

Whether you are booking a new appointment or renewing your NBI certificate Online, the official Website is a reliable platform in both cases. Here’s the link for an online NBI Appointment.

⚠️

Note: Some applicants mistakenly search for “clearance nbi.gov.com.ph” — the correct official domain is clearance.nbi.gov.ph. Always double-check the URL before entering your details.

➜Login

  • Open the default web browser on your Smartphone or PC and visit the Official Website.
  • You can also click the “Above Button” to directly land on the Official Application Page.
  • Notably, first-time job seekers can also follow this: https://firsttimejobseekers.nbi.gov.ph/ for online booking but they must first fulfil the necessary requirements.
  • On the NBI Government application page, applicants will need to answer the following question: “Do you have an Old NBI Clearance issue from 2014 to Present?”
  • Since you’re claiming a new NBI Clearance, select “No”.
  • Now, start filling out the Registration Form.
  • Provide correct personal details, and tick the box saying, “Read and Accept Terms of Services”.
  • Next, complete the security option by clicking “I’m not a robot”.
  • Then, click the “Signup” button.

OTP Verification

After completing the Signup process, the system will authenticate your identity through a One-time PIN. Therefore, provide a verified mobile number during signup and verify the NBI OTP within the given time.

  • After successful registration, this System will send an OTP to your provided Mobile Number.
  • Input the six-digit code on the Portal to verify your account.
  • Once your Account is verified, the system will redirect you to the Application Information Page.
  • Fill out the required fields, including civil status, birthplace, and permanent address.
  • Next, click the “Save Information” button, and review your Application Form carefully.
  • Applicants can edit the Form by clicking the “Edit Information” option.
  • Once satisfied, click “Apply For Clearance”.

ID Verifications

In the next phase, the Portal will ask applicants to select the available IDs for verification. Of course, applicants should bring the selected IDs during their scheduled Appointment in the NBI Office.

  • Afterwards, the system will ask you to select NBI Valid IDs that you shall represent at the NBI Office.
  • The available NBI IDs include Passports, Driver’s Licenses, National IDs, PRC IDs, and UMIDs.
  • Applicants should provide these IDs during NBI Branch Appearance.

Appointment Booking

After ID verification, you’ll pick your preferred NBI branch, an available date (highlighted in blue), and a time slot. The system then shows your appointment details before you continue to payment.

For the full step-by-step booking process, rescheduling instructions, and what to expect on appointment day, see our complete NBI appointment guide.

Fee Payment

Afterward, the Online Portal will request applicants to pay the Clearance Fee before their Online Appointment. After successful payment, the system will generate a Payment Receipt.

  • After setting your appointment, the system will also ask you to select a payment method for the Fee.
  • It supports Bank over the Counter, Online Bank, Bayad Center Outlets, Bayad Center Mobile App, ECPay, and 7-Eleven.
  • Choose your preferred Payment Method, and click “Proceed”.
  • After deducting Fees, the system will provide you with a Reference Number For Payment.
  • Notably, the Fee is PHP 130 plus a service charge of about PHP 25–30 for E-payments, including 7-Eleven, Visa, Mastercard, or GCash.

Branch Visit

Lastly, applicants should visit the NBI Branch on the day of their scheduled Appointment. Dress formally, be neat, and confidently answer all related queries to the NBI representative.

  • After confirming your Appointment, visit the NBI Branch on the scheduled date and time.
  • Bring along your selected Verification Documents alongside the Appointment Reference Number and Payment Receipt.
  • Next, tell the NBI staff about the Appointment.
  • Provide your documents and complete the remaining Process, including Biometric captures, Photo Capture, and Final Verification.
  • Finally, visit the Portal to check your Status.

NBI Branch Application

NBI operates through nationwide NBI Branches and Satellite Offices. In addition to NBI-related support and customer services, NBI Regional Branches also assist applicants in applying for the Certificate.

  • Visit your nearby NBI Branch.
  • Request the official representative for NBI Appointment Form.
  • Fill out the application form manually.
  • Present valid IDs for verification.
  • Pay the clearance fee at the cashier.
  • Proceed to biometrics, photo capture, and data encoding.
  • Shortly, you’ll receive NBI Tracking Number on your provided mobile number.

Renewal Process

NBI also offers streamlined channels for renewal process. To renew the Clearance, existing users can provide their Old NBI Number and choose delivery service without paying additional postal charges.

  • Navigate to NBI Official Government Portal Online.
  • Open the Renewal Section.
  • Fill out the renewal form online.
  • Pay the processing and courier fees.
  • Wait for the clearance to be delivered to your address.

Clearance Fee for NBI (2026)

The clearance fee is ₱130. Online payment adds a small e-payment service charge of about ₱25–₱30, so most applicants pay ₱155 in total (up to ₱160 on some channels). The fee is the same for new applications and renewals, and it does not depend on your purpose. First-time job seekers can apply free under RA 11261.

Check Your NBI Clearance Status

After applying, the system provides an NBI Application Tracking Number. Meanwhile, applicants can use the NBI Reference Number to check their NBI Application Status and act accordingly.

  • Go to the official NBI online portal: clearance.nbi.gov.ph.
  • Enter the email address and password you used during registration.
  • Locate the “Check Status” or “Application Tracking” section on the portal.
  • Enter the NBI Reference Number exactly as shown.
  • Complete the CAPTCHA or any security verification.
  • Click the “Search,” “Check Status,” or equivalent button to submit your query.

How To Reschedule NBI Appointment

Applicants can change or cancel their NBI Appointment. Although the process is simple, applicants must complete the official cancellation requirements to reschedule their NBI Appointment without losing their payment or record.

  • Log in to your NBI account using your registered email and password.
  • Go to “My Appointments” or the section where your existing booking is listed.
  • Click the option to view appointment details.
  • Select the Cancel / Reschedule button to begin the cancellation process.
  • Follow the on-screen cancellation prompts.
  • Confirm the cancellation.
  • Next, proceed to book a new appointment using the same Reference Number.
  • Select your preferred NBI branch, date, and time from the available slots.

NBI Clearance Status Meanings

Applicants can track its progress through the Online Portal. In the meantime, the following section discusses the official meanings of different Statuses.

Release Status

If the NBI Portal shows “For Release” status, it means that your Application has been processed and is ready for claiming. Afterwards, applicants can visit the NBI Branch to collect the certificate or wait for NBI Postal Delivery.

In Process Status

The “In Process” status indicates that the application is still under verification by the system. Applicants should keep checking the portal until the status updates.

HIT Status

When an applicant’s name or record matches someone else’s record, the system displays “Hit” status. Officials may manually verify your identity before releasing the certificate.

Invalid Status

When an Application has incomplete information, invalid payments, or false details, the portal displays “Invalid or Incorrect”. Recheck your information or contact NBI support.

Benefits of NBI Philippines Clearance in 2026

It offers equal benefits for authorities and citizens. Law enforcement agencies gain streamlined access to criminal records, while applicants can perform various tasks using this Certificate.

Good Citizen Proof

It checks an individual’s criminal history through major Philippine law-enforcement databases and serves as proof for citizens with no convictions.

Multi-Purpose Use

Locals and foreigners present their clearance validity certificates for visa applications, business, employment, and study scholarships.

Digitalized Appointments

International Access

Permanent Identity

Downsides of NBI Clearance

The Online Website is optimized and user-friendly, but it has several access-related limitations.

Time-Bar Validity

The Certificate is valid for one year. Applicants must renew it and pay the applicable fee again.

Offline Dependency

Online steps cover the Appointment Form, booking, and payment, but applicants may still need a branch visit.

Single Country Validity

Requirements for immigration, work, or study abroad may include a separate clearance from another country.

Data Privacy Risks

Any online system storing sensitive personal information carries privacy risks despite security controls.
