1. What is NBI Clearance?

NBI Clearance is a certificate issued by the National Bureau of Investigation after checking an applicant’s identifying information against NBI records. In the Philippines, employers and other organizations commonly request it as part of background, employment, licensing, travel or documentation requirements.

This website is an independent guide. It explains the current NBI Clearance online application, appointment, renewal, requirements, fees, HIT status, eGovPH eClearance and special routes such as first-time job seekers and applicants abroad. The actual application, payment and issuance remain with the NBI.

Use the official system. For any real NBI transaction, start from clearance.nbi.gov.ph or an official NBI page.

2. How to apply for NBI Clearance online

The current NBI process starts online. Register or log in, complete your profile, apply for clearance, choose a branch and schedule, then pay using a supported channel. After the transaction is marked paid, go to the selected branch for biometrics and record verification.

1Register or log inUse the official NBI Clearance Application Portal and verify the account with your active contact details.
2Complete your profileEnter personal, family and employment/education information carefully. Match your IDs.
3Apply & scheduleChoose the valid ID you will bring, select a branch, then pick an available AM or PM slot.
4Pay the feeCurrent NBI guidance lists a ₱130 basic fee plus a small e-payment service charge. Save the reference number.
5Visit the branchBring the required IDs and proof of payment for photo, fingerprints, signature, verification and release.
Missed the exact appointment date? The NBI’s current how-to page says recent system updates allow a 15-day grace period after a transaction is marked PAID. Because portal behavior can change, check the live transaction status and your branch instructions before relying on the grace period.

3. NBI Clearance requirements

For a standard branch visit, current NBI guidance says to bring your reference number or QR code, proof of payment, and two original, unexpired government-issued IDs. Requirements change for special routes, so first-time job seekers and applicants abroad should use their dedicated checklists.

4. Valid IDs accepted for NBI Clearance

The current NBI how-to guide gives examples including a Philippine Passport, Driver’s License, National ID, UMID, Postal ID and PSA Birth Certificate, while the Citizen’s Charter states that two government-issued identification cards are required for the standard process.

Do not rely on a one-ID shortcut. Some third-party pages say one ID is enough. Current official NBI guidance we reviewed points to two valid IDs for the standard application, so bringing two original, unexpired IDs is the safer rule.

5. NBI Clearance fee and payment methods

The NBI’s current how-to page lists a ₱130 basic clearance fee plus a minimal e-payment service charge, usually around ₱25–₱30. It names GCash, Maya, 7-Eleven, Bayad Center and Online Banking as payment options.

Why do some official pages show different amounts? An older NBI Citizen’s Charter page still displays legacy fee figures. For a live payment, use the amount generated by the current official NBI Clearance portal, not an old screenshot or third-party total.

7. What does a HIT mean in NBI Clearance?

A “HIT” means the NBI system found a possible match that requires further verification before the clearance can be released. A HIT does not automatically mean that you have a criminal record. The NBI’s current how-to guide explains that a similar or identical name can trigger manual review.

Typical review window in current NBI guidance. The current NBI how-to page describes a usual return period of around 5 to 10 working days for a HIT. Follow the exact date and instructions given by the branch handling your application.

8. NBI Clearance renewal guide

Renewal is not one single route in 2026. The current NBI site says an online renewal option is available for certain clearances issued after 2014 where personal details have not changed, while eGovPH now adds a digital renewal path for eligible verified users. Applicants abroad also have a separate mailed-renewal process.

10. NBI Clearance for applicants abroad / OFWs

If you are outside the Philippines, do not assume the normal local branch process applies. NBI publishes a separate mailed-clearance procedure. For applications with a designated representative, the representative must register the application online, choose the NBI Main Clearance Center, complete payment and bring the required documents to the NBI Mailed Clearance Section.

Official processing note. NBI says applications from abroad are processed only at the NBI Clearance Building on U.N. Avenue, Ermita, Manila, and states a maximum processing time of five working days upon receipt of documents.

14. NBI Clearance frequently asked questions

Where do I apply for NBI Clearance online?

Use the official NBI Clearance Application Portal at clearance.nbi.gov.ph. This website provides independent guidance only.

How much is NBI Clearance?

Current NBI how-to guidance lists a ₱130 basic fee plus a minimal e-payment service charge, usually around ₱25–₱30. Confirm the final amount generated by the official system.

How many IDs should I bring?

Current NBI guidance says standard applicants should bring two original, unexpired government-issued IDs.

What if my NBI Clearance has a HIT?

Follow the return date and verification instructions from the branch. A HIT requires further checking and does not by itself prove a criminal record.

Can I renew through eGovPH?

NBI launched eClearance on eGovPH in July 2026. Official NBI information says eligible verified users can renew and access a digital copy through the app.

Can I apply from abroad?

Yes. NBI publishes a separate mailed-clearance process for applicants outside the Philippines.
