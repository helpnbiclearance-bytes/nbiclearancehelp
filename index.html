Why NBI Clearance Needed

This Online Portal is an impactful step by the Philippine Government to cope with increasing crimes, drugs, and fraudulent activities. Of course, the process helps authorities to track and hunt individuals with criminal records.

Legitimate Document

The NBI Online Application Form is a legitimate document issued by the National Bureau of Investigation under the Department of Justice. Notably, the NBI Database verifies each NBI appointment application before approval.

National Coverage

NBI Record Check is not limited to the NBI. In practice, all Clearance Applications cross-match the databases of all major law enforcement agencies, including the Philippine National Police, the Jail Management, and the Drug Enforcement.

Digital Record System

It’s impossible to bypass or forge the NBI Appointment Registration, as the entire system is digitized. NBI authorities store the applicant’s private details, biometrics, and photos in their end-to-end encrypted database.

Official Validity Period

This Online Application Form has a 1-year expiration date. Eventually, applicants need a new Appointment for NBI Renewal, and this method helps NBI authorities access up-to-date records and statuses for individuals.

Unique Tracking IDs

NBI assigns a unique ID number for each application. In the meantime, applicants can access the official website, provide their tracking IDs, and check their current NBI status without hassle.

What is Purpose of NBI Clearance Philippines

This Appointment new is mandatory for local and overseas individuals for all legitimate reasons. Indeed, the Philippine Government has the authority to deny or seize several Civil Rights on the grounds of NBI Clearance.

1🔍

Record Verification

The foremost reason to Apply For this process online is to verify an individual’s criminal record through the Government database. Of course, the portal is among the notable initiatives by the Philippine Government to maintain law and order.

2💼

Employment

All citizens should submit the Application for employment purposes in the Philippines or Overseas. In practice, this process ensures job access to applicants with spotless criminal records.

3📄

Transactions

Before engaging in Government-based transactions for civil service exams, visa applications, and work permits, applicants should set an NBI Appointment Schedule. Otherwise, the concerned departments can reject your applications.

4✈️

Travelers

Travelers, tourists, and foreigners in the Philippines should schedule their Appointment Online Schedule before interacting with the Immigration Department. The Government may deny Visas, Study Plans, or Business Ventures for foreigners without this Clearance.

5🏢

Business

Before applying for Business Permits, Firearms Licenses, Driver’s Licenses, or other Legal Activities, applicants should visit the NBI Online Website or a physical branch and claim their NBI Clearance Certificates.

Tips for a Smooth Application

  • Type your name exactly as it appears on your valid IDs — mismatches cause most delays.
  • Use your own active mobile number and email address; OTPs and updates go there.
  • Screenshot or save your payment reference number immediately after paying.
  • Bring two original, unexpired IDs to the branch — photocopies will not do.
  • Arrive early for your appointment and dress appropriately for an ID photo.
  • Never share your OTP or account password with anyone, including people who offer to "help".

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I apply for NBI Clearance online?

Use the official NBI Clearance Application Portal at clearance.nbi.gov.ph. This website provides independent guidance only.

How much is NBI Clearance?

Current NBI how-to guidance lists a ₱130 basic fee plus a minimal e-payment service charge, usually around ₱25–₱30. Confirm the final amount generated by the official system.

How many IDs should I bring?

Current NBI guidance says standard applicants should bring two original, unexpired government-issued IDs.

What if my NBI Clearance has a HIT?

Follow the return date and verification instructions from the branch. A HIT requires further checking and does not by itself prove a criminal record.

Can I renew through eGovPH?

NBI launched eClearance on eGovPH in July 2026. Official NBI information says eligible verified users can renew and access a digital copy through the app.

Can I apply from abroad?

Yes. NBI publishes a separate mailed-clearance process for applicants outside the Philippines.
