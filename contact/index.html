Current official NBI contact page

The NBI contact page currently lists the Main Office at Filinvest Cyberzone Bay, Diosdado Macapagal Boulevard, Pasay City and the NBI Clearance Center at United Nations Avenue, Ermita, Manila. It also publishes current clearance-inquiry, clearance-delivery and mailed-clearance contacts.
