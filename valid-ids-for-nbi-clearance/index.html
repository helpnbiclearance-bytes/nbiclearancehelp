Valid IDs for NBI Clearance

Bring two original, unexpired government-issued IDs for the standard NBI Clearance process unless your official applicant route specifies a different document set.

Official NBI Portal ↗

How many IDs do you need?

The NBI Citizen’s Charter says the standard clearance service requires two valid identification cards issued by the government. The current How to Apply page also tells branch visitors to bring two original, unexpired IDs.

Safer rule: bring two. Even if you see a third-party article claiming one ID is enough, the current official sources we reviewed support bringing two valid IDs for the standard route.

First-Time Job Seekers

The FTJS Citizen’s Charter requires the Barangay Certification and two valid government IDs or acceptable certificates. This is a separate program-specific checklist.

How to choose the two IDs you will bring

Choose documents that are current, original and consistent with the identity details in your NBI profile. If one document shows a different name format, old civil status or other discrepancy, bringing a second clean supporting ID can help the branch understand the situation, but the NBI still controls what it accepts.

National ID / PhilID

The current NBI how-to page lists National ID among its examples. If you use it, make sure the identity details match the information saved in your NBI account.

PSA Birth Certificate

The same current how-to page includes a PSA Birth Certificate among examples. Because a birth certificate is not the same type of photo ID as a passport or driver’s license, applicants with unusual document combinations should follow the latest branch instructions rather than relying on a third-party one-size-fits-all list.

Check document quality, not only the ID name

An ID type appearing in an example list does not help if the document is expired, damaged, unreadable or conflicts with the application. Prepare two strong originals and examine them before the appointment.

If the names on your IDs differ

Do not guess how a maiden name, married name, suffix, missing middle name or spelling difference will be resolved. Bring the strongest supporting civil or legal document available and ask the official NBI channel or branch what your situation requires. This independent site cannot approve an ID combination in advance.

Valid ID FAQs

Can I bring only one valid ID?

Our recommendation is no for the standard route. The official sources we reviewed say two government-issued IDs are required.

Can I use an expired ID?

Current NBI guidance says the IDs should be original and not expired.

Can first-time job seekers use certificates?

The FTJS Citizen’s Charter says two valid government IDs or acceptable certificates may be used alongside the required Barangay Certification.
