Corrections & Updates Policy

How NBI Clearance Help handles factual corrections, outdated procedures and source conflicts.

Last verified: 14 September 2026Official-source checkedPhilippines-focused
Official NBI Portal ↗

NBI Clearance procedures can change. Our corrections process is designed to protect users from outdated instructions.

When we update

We update a page when an official NBI source changes a fee, requirement, portal flow, office detail, digital-service capability or other fact that can affect a user’s next step.

How conflicts are handled

If two official pages disagree, we do not silently hide the older page. We explain the conflict, prioritize the live/current transaction flow where appropriate and link both official sources so readers can verify.

High-risk corrections

Payment, account security, identity documents and HIT guidance receive extra caution because bad advice can create financial or privacy problems.
