No NBI Appointment Slots Available? Try These Steps

Use the live official portal to compare dates and available branch choices. A branch can exist without having an appointment slot available for the date you need.

By NBI Clearance Help Editorial TeamUpdated 11 October 2026
Open the official NBI portal ↗

Quick answer

Check the live calendar rather than a claimed release schedule. Try another available date, compare practical branch options inside the official transaction, and verify unfamiliar locations in the official NBI locator. Do not buy a slot from a third party.

Why a branch may show no available slots

The branch may have no selectable capacity for the dates currently displayed, the portal may be busy, or the calendar may not have loaded correctly. This does not necessarily mean that the office has closed or that your account is permanently blocked.

No official rule guarantees that slots for every branch appear at a particular hour. Continue from the NBI appointment guide and use only options shown in the live transaction.

Full vs broken: reading the calendar correctly

A calendar that loads and shows dates you cannot select is usually a capacity problem — the branch is full for the displayed period. A calendar that will not load, shows errors, or logs you out is usually a technical problem. The fixes differ, so identify which one you are seeing before acting.

Steps to try when no appointment date is available

  1. Review other dates shown for the same branch.
  2. Check another branch that is practical for you and available in the official transaction.
  3. Use the NBI Clearance branches guide and official locator to verify the office before choosing it.
  4. Confirm that your profile and application step are complete.
  5. If the calendar appears broken, save relevant transaction information and retry safely.
An office listing is not a slot guarantee. A branch can appear in the official locator but have no online appointment capacity for the selected date.

Keep your profile booking-ready

An incomplete profile or unfinished application step can hide the calendar entirely. Before concluding that no slots exist, confirm your registration details are complete and your transaction has reached the scheduling step — then recheck.

Branch strategy when your preferred branch is full

Compare nearby branches, not just dates

Applicants often check every date at one branch and never look at the next-nearest office. Open the branch options inside your official transaction and compare two or three practical locations. Verify each candidate in the official NBI locator before selecting it, and weigh travel time against waiting weeks for your first choice.

Recheck at sensible intervals

Slot availability changes as other applicants reschedule or cancel. Rechecking the live portal at different times of day is reasonable — but this guide does not promise a secret release hour, and anyone selling one is not being honest. Occasional checks beat constant refreshing, which can cause session errors.

If the calendar or date selector is not working

  • Confirm that you are using the genuine `clearance.nbi.gov.ph` portal.
  • Reload once and sign in again through the official page if the session has expired.
  • Try a current browser or another trusted device.
  • Disable only browser extensions that clearly interfere with page controls; do not install an unknown “NBI helper.”
  • Try another trusted network if the portal is not loading.
  • Use the NBI login troubleshooting guide if the problem begins before the calendar.
  • Check the Help Center if another transaction problem appears.

Do not fight the portal with workarounds

Unofficial "helper" extensions, auto-refresh scripts and third-party booking tools can compromise your account or get it flagged. If the official calendar genuinely will not work after the steps above, the safe move is to wait and retry — not to hand your login to a stranger's tool.

What to save before retrying

  • Your account email or mobile number—kept private.
  • Any reference number already generated.
  • The branch and date options you were considering.
  • A screenshot of the error that does not expose passwords, OTPs or full identity information.
  • Payment proof if payment has already occurred.

When to stop retrying and ask for help

If the calendar works but shows no availability across every practical branch and date for an extended period, or if a technical error persists after the troubleshooting above, continued retrying is unlikely to change the outcome on its own. At that point, use official NBI support channels with your saved transaction details rather than repeating the same steps. Account-specific availability questions cannot be answered by an independent guide.

Eligible for renewal? Some applicants renewing a previous clearance may qualify for the eGovPH eClearance route instead of a branch visit — check the eGovPH renewal guide to see whether it applies to you.

What not to do

  • Do not pay someone who promises a hidden or guaranteed slot.
  • Do not share your password or OTP with a slot seller.
  • Do not create many accounts to keep checking the same calendar.
  • Do not assume a third-party map listing proves online availability.
  • Do not promise yourself a specific slot-release time based on an old post.

No-slot FAQs

When do new NBI appointment slots open?

There is no reliable universal release time published for every branch. Check the live official portal and do not pay anyone who claims to know a guaranteed secret schedule.

Can I choose another NBI branch if there are no slots?

Check the other branch options available in your official transaction. Verify a location in the official NBI locator before selecting it, because an office listing does not guarantee that an online slot is currently available.

What if the NBI appointment calendar is not working?

Save any reference or completed profile information, reload the official portal once, try a current browser or another trusted connection, and retry later if the service appears unavailable. Do not create repeated accounts as a first response.

Can I walk in to an NBI branch if no online slots are available?

Do not assume walk-in processing is accepted. Follow the appointment and branch instructions shown in your live official transaction before travelling.

Why do NBI slots seem to disappear so fast?

Branches have limited daily capacity and demand is high, so visible dates can fill quickly. This guide does not state a guaranteed release time — keep checking the live portal and compare other dates or branches.

How can I tell if the portal is down or just fully booked?

If the calendar loads but shows no selectable dates, the branch is likely full for the displayed period. If pages fail to load, show errors or will not sign you in, treat it as a technical problem and retry later using the troubleshooting steps on this page.

Should I keep refreshing the calendar all day?

Occasional rechecks are reasonable, but constant refreshing can trigger session problems. Check at sensible intervals, compare other branches and dates, and keep your profile complete so you can book immediately when a slot appears.
