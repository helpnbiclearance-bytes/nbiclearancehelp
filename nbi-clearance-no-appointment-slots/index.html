No NBI Appointment Slots Available? Try These Steps

Use the live official portal to compare dates and available branch choices. A branch can exist without having an appointment slot available for the date you need.

Open the official NBI portal ↗

Quick answer

Check the live calendar rather than a claimed release schedule. Try another available date, compare practical branch options inside the official transaction, and verify unfamiliar locations in the official NBI locator. Do not buy a slot from a third party.

Why a branch may show no available slots

The branch may have no selectable capacity for the dates currently displayed, the portal may be busy, or the calendar may not have loaded correctly. This does not necessarily mean that the office has closed or that your account is permanently blocked.

No official rule guarantees that slots for every branch appear at a particular hour. Continue from the NBI appointment guide and use only options shown in the live transaction.

Steps to try when no appointment date is available

  1. Review other dates shown for the same branch.
  2. Check another branch that is practical for you and available in the official transaction.
  3. Use the NBI Clearance branches guide and official locator to verify the office before choosing it.
  4. Confirm that your profile and application step are complete.
  5. If the calendar appears broken, save relevant transaction information and retry safely.
An office listing is not a slot guarantee. A branch can appear in the official locator but have no online appointment capacity for the selected date.

If the calendar or date selector is not working

  • Confirm that you are using the genuine `clearance.nbi.gov.ph` portal.
  • Reload once and sign in again through the official page if the session has expired.
  • Try a current browser or another trusted device.
  • Disable only browser extensions that clearly interfere with page controls; do not install an unknown “NBI helper.”
  • Try another trusted network if the portal is not loading.
  • Use the NBI login troubleshooting guide if the problem begins before the calendar.
  • Check the Help Center if another transaction problem appears.

What to save before retrying

  • Your account email or mobile number—kept private.
  • Any reference number already generated.
  • The branch and date options you were considering.
  • A screenshot of the error that does not expose passwords, OTPs or full identity information.
  • Payment proof if payment has already occurred.

What not to do

  • Do not pay someone who promises a hidden or guaranteed slot.
  • Do not share your password or OTP with a slot seller.
  • Do not create many accounts to keep checking the same calendar.
  • Do not assume a third-party map listing proves online availability.
  • Do not promise yourself a specific slot-release time based on an old post.

No-slot FAQs

When do new NBI appointment slots open?

There is no reliable universal release time published for every branch. Check the live official portal and do not pay anyone who claims to know a guaranteed secret schedule.

Can I choose another NBI branch if there are no slots?

Check the other branch options available in your official transaction. Verify a location in the official NBI locator before selecting it, because an office listing does not guarantee that an online slot is currently available.

What if the NBI appointment calendar is not working?

Save any reference or completed profile information, reload the official portal once, try a current browser or another trusted connection, and retry later if the service appears unavailable. Do not create repeated accounts as a first response.
