Before you go

  • Complete your online application.
  • Pay through an official supported channel.
  • Save your reference number / QR code and receipt.
  • Bring the required valid IDs and any program-specific documents.
  • Verify current office information before travel.

NBI Clearance Center contact

The NBI contact page currently lists the NBI Clearance Center at the NBI Clearance Building, United Nations Avenue, Ermita, Manila, with operating hours Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM, plus clearance inquiry contacts on the official page.

Why we do not publish dozens of thin city pages

Office locations can change, and a copied city page becomes harmful when it sends people to an outdated address. Instead, this site keeps branch guidance in one nationwide hub and points users to the official NBI locator for live location checking.

What to verify before travelling

  • The branch still appears on the official NBI locator.
  • Your transaction points to the branch you intend to visit.
  • You have already completed the online steps and payment that apply to your route.
  • You have your IDs and program-specific documents.

Branch FAQs

Where is the NBI Clearance Center in Manila?

The current NBI contact page lists the NBI Clearance Building at United Nations Avenue, Ermita, Manila. Use the official page for current contact information and hours.

Should I go to a branch before paying?

The official NBI office locator tells applicants to apply and pay online before going to a clearance office.

Does this website maintain separate Manila, Cebu or Davao SEO pages?

No. We use one branch hub and the official locator so location guidance stays focused and easier to maintain.
