Before You Visit an NBI Branch

  • Complete your online application.
  • Pay through an official supported channel.
  • Save your reference number / QR code and receipt.
  • Bring the required valid IDs and any program-specific documents.
  • Verify current office information before travel.

Why We Use the Official NBI Locator

A clearance center may move, pause service or stop appearing in the live transaction system while an old address remains in search results, copied directories or map listings. The official locator and the options shown in your transaction are safer starting points than a static nationwide address list.

What to verify before travelling

  • The branch still appears on the official NBI locator.
  • Your transaction points to the branch you intend to visit.
  • You have already completed the online steps and payment that apply to your route.
  • You have your IDs and program-specific documents.

Choose a practical clearance office

The nearest pin on a map is not always the best option. Compare the offices offered in your live transaction, travel time, available target windows and accessibility needs. Select the office inside the official system; an address shown in an old post does not prove that the branch is currently available.

Branch FAQs

Can I get NBI Clearance anywhere?

Do not assume you can walk into any office. Choose from the branch options available in your official NBI transaction and follow the selected branch and schedule instructions.

Can I choose any NBI branch?

Choose from the branch options available in your official transaction. Before travel, verify the selected office in the official NBI locator and follow the schedule or instructions shown for your transaction.

Do I need an appointment before visiting an NBI branch?

The current normal flow emphasizes completing the online application, selecting a branch and schedule, and paying before travel. Follow the live NBI portal and your selected branch instructions because official guidance and branch procedures can change. See the NBI appointment guide for the booking steps.

Where is the NBI Clearance Center in Manila?

The NBI contact page reviewed on 14 September 2026 lists the NBI Clearance Building at United Nations Avenue, Ermita, Manila. Verify the address and hours on the official contact page before travel, and use the official locator for other Manila-area options.

Why doesn’t this website list every NBI branch address?

Branch details can change, and copied directories become outdated. This independent guide uses one nationwide preparation page and directs visitors to the official NBI locator for current locations.
