How the official NBI office locator works

The NBI maintains a clearance office locator on its official website. It is the only branch list that the NBI itself keeps current, which is why this guide points you there instead of maintaining a competing directory.

  1. Open the official locator link above on nbi.gov.ph.
  2. Browse or search for the area you need — the locator organizes offices so you can find options near you.
  3. Note the office name, address, and any hours or notes shown.
  4. Cross-check: open your live NBI transaction and confirm the same office appears among your selectable branch options.
  5. If the two disagree, trust the transaction options and official notices over any third-party listing.
Bookmark both pages. You will want the locator (to find offices) and your transaction page (to book one) open side by side when you finalize your choice.

Before You Visit an NBI Branch

Most wasted branch trips come from arriving unprepared, not from the branch itself. Run through this checklist before you travel:

  • Complete your online application. The current normal flow starts on the NBI clearance application portal — the branch visit comes after the online steps, not instead of them.
  • Pay through an official supported channel. Use only the channels shown in your transaction, and keep the receipt.
  • Save your reference number / QR code and receipt. Screenshot them and keep a backup — you will need them at the branch and again if anything goes wrong.
  • Bring the required valid IDs and any program-specific documents. Check the NBI clearance requirements and what to bring guides so you do not get sent home for a missing ID.
  • Verify current office information before travel. Re-check the locator and your transaction on the day — branches can and do change schedules.

NBI Clearance Branches in Manila

The NBI contact page reviewed on 14 September 2026 lists the NBI Clearance Center at the NBI Clearance Building, United Nations Avenue, Ermita, Manila, with operating hours Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Verify the address and hours on the official contact page before travel because operational details can change. For other Manila-area options, use the official NBI office locator and confirm the branch shown in your live transaction.

As the main clearance center, the Manila office handles high applicant volumes — expect longer queues than at smaller offices, especially on Mondays and after holidays. If your transaction offers a nearer or less busy alternative, the travel time you save is rarely worth less than the waiting time you avoid.

Branches outside Metro Manila

NBI clearance services are not limited to Manila — offices operate across the country’s regions, and the official locator is how you find the current ones near you. This guide deliberately does not publish a static nationwide address list: branch details change, and copied directories go stale quietly while still ranking in search results.

If you are applying from a province, plan around two realities. First, fewer offices serve larger areas, so the nearest bookable branch may still be a real trip — verify it in your transaction before committing to travel. Second, provincial schedules can shift around local holidays and weather disruptions, so re-confirm the office the day before you go. The preparation checklist above applies everywhere; distance just raises the cost of getting it wrong.

Why We Use the Official NBI Locator

A clearance center may move, pause service or stop appearing in the live transaction system while an old address remains in search results, copied directories or map listings. The official locator and the options shown in your transaction are safer starting points than a static nationwide address list.

This is a deliberate editorial choice, documented in our editorial policy: we would rather send you to one live official source than maintain a directory we cannot personally re-verify office by office. A wrong address on our page could cost you a day’s travel; a link to the locator cannot.

What to verify before travelling

  • The branch still appears on the official NBI locator.
  • Your transaction points to the branch you intend to visit.
  • You have already completed the online steps and payment that apply to your route.
  • You have your IDs and program-specific documents.

The nearest pin on a map is not always the best option. Compare the offices offered in your live transaction, travel time, available target windows and accessibility needs. How to pick between two nearby offices

When your transaction offers more than one branch, weigh travel time against expected crowding. Main and city-center offices usually process more applicants per day but also draw longer lines; smaller offices may be calmer but have tighter schedules. If you have accessibility needs — step-free access, seating while waiting — factor that in before you commit, and confirm any specific concern through official channels rather than assuming.

What to expect on your branch visit

Branch procedures can change, so treat this as a general orientation, not a script. A standard visit usually follows this shape:

  1. Check-in with your reference. Present your reference number or QR code plus your IDs at the reception or encoding area.
  2. Identity verification and biometrics capture. Expect a photo and fingerprint capture as part of the standard identity process.
  3. Record check. Your details go through the NBI’s record matching — this is where a HIT status would surface if your name matches a record needing review.
  4. Release or further instruction. Clear applications proceed to printing and release; flagged ones receive instructions on what to bring or when to return.

Processing time varies with queue length and record results. Bring patience, water, and everything on your checklist; the applicants who finish fastest are the ones who arrived complete.

Same-day release is common for straightforward cases — but it is never guaranteed. Do not book non-refundable travel around an assumed release time.

When the branch you want isn’t available

Sometimes the office you want does not appear in your transaction, shows no appointment slots, or disappears from the locator. Do not panic and do not pay anyone who promises to “open” a slot.

  • Pick the next-best branch in your transaction. Only offices the system offers you are bookable — an office you cannot select is not an option, however close it is.
  • Check official notices. Temporary closures, holiday schedules, and system maintenance are announced through official channels; a missing branch is often temporary.
  • Consider the renewal shortcut. If you are renewing an eligible existing clearance, the eGovPH eClearance route may remove the branch visit entirely.
  • Missed your schedule? Read the missed appointment guide before rebooking — your transaction may still be usable.

Branch FAQs

Can I get NBI Clearance anywhere?

Do not assume you can walk into any office. Choose from the branch options available in your official NBI transaction and follow the selected branch and schedule instructions.

Can I choose any NBI branch?

Choose from the branch options available in your official transaction. Before travel, verify the selected office in the official NBI locator and follow the schedule or instructions shown for your transaction.

Do I need an appointment before visiting an NBI branch?

The current normal flow emphasizes completing the online application, selecting a branch and schedule, and paying before travel. Follow the live NBI portal and your selected branch instructions because official guidance and branch procedures can change. See the NBI appointment guide for the booking steps.

Where is the NBI Clearance Center in Manila?

The NBI contact page reviewed on 14 September 2026 lists the NBI Clearance Building at United Nations Avenue, Ermita, Manila. Verify the address and hours on the official contact page before travel, and use the official locator for other Manila-area options.

Why doesn’t this website list every NBI branch address?

Branch details can change, and copied directories become outdated. This independent guide uses one nationwide preparation page and directs visitors to the official NBI locator for current locations.

What are the usual NBI branch operating hours?

The Manila clearance center listing reviewed shows Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM, but hours vary by office and can change. Verify the hours for your specific branch before travelling.

Is there an NBI branch in my province or city?

Use the official NBI office locator to check current options near you. This site does not keep a static branch directory because copied address lists become outdated.

Can I change my selected branch after paying?

Branch selection is tied to your transaction, and the guidance reviewed does not document a self-service branch change after payment. Check your portal options or official help channels before assuming a switch is possible.

Do NBI branches accept walk-in applicants?

The current normal flow is online application plus schedule selection before visiting. Walk-in availability is not documented as the standard route, so complete the online steps first.
