How to Verify an NBI Clearance Online

The official NBI Clearance portal includes an Online Verification service for checking a clearance’s authenticity and validity. Use that official service, compare its result with the document presented, and protect the clearance holder’s personal information.

By NBI Clearance Help Editorial TeamUpdated 11 October 2026
Open Official NBI Portal ↗ See verification steps

Quick answer

Go directly to clearance.nbi.gov.ph and choose NBI Clearance Online Verification under NBI Clearance eServices. The official portal says this service checks a clearance’s authenticity and validity.

If you are checking a clearance because it is about to expire, read the NBI Clearance renewal guide to understand validity periods and renewal routes before you rely on the document.

Use only the official NBI domain. This independent website does not verify clearances and must not receive a clearance number, QR code, ID image, fingerprints, date of birth, password or OTP.

How to verify NBI Clearance online

Online verification is used by clearance holders checking their own document, and by employers, schools or agencies confirming a document presented to them. The process is the same in both cases: only the official NBI portal can confirm what the NBI has on record.

Before you start

  • Have the clearance document ready. Work from the original document or a clear, complete copy so you can read every character exactly as printed.
  • Use a private, secure device. Avoid entering clearance or identity details on shared or public computers, and avoid saving the document in shared folders or chat apps.
  • Confirm the official domain first. The verification service lives at clearance.nbi.gov.ph. Type the address yourself or use a bookmarked official link instead of clicking links from messages, comments or emails.
  1. Open the official portal yourself. Type clearance.nbi.gov.ph into the browser or use the official link above. Check the domain before entering any document information.
  2. Find NBI Clearance eServices. On the public portal, select NBI Clearance Online Verification.
  3. Use the details requested by the live service. Read them carefully from the clearance being checked. The portal interface can change, so follow the current official prompts rather than an old screenshot or third-party form.
  4. Review the official response. Check whether the service confirms the record and compare the displayed information with the clearance presented to you.
  5. Check the document itself. Confirm that the name and relevant details match, the document is readable and its printed validity or expiration information meets the requesting organization’s rules.

After you see the result

If the portal confirms the record and the details match the document, the check is complete. What happens next depends on the requesting organization’s own process and recency rules — the verification result confirms document information; it does not make an employment, licensing or visa decision for you.

If you are checking your own newly claimed clearance and no record appears, wait and retry later before assuming a problem. Portal availability and record visibility can vary, and a single failed attempt is not proof that anything is wrong. The NBI Clearance processing time guide explains the usual release flow after a branch visit.

For employers and organizations: verification is one control, not permission to collect or retain more personal information than necessary. Limit access to authorized staff and follow applicable privacy rules.

How employers and agencies typically use the result

Most organizations use verification as one step in a wider document check. They compare the official portal response with the clearance presented, confirm that the name and other material details agree, and check the validity information against their own recency rules — for example, whether they require a clearance issued within the last six or twelve months. Keep the check minimal: asking for the document is not a reason to collect or store more personal information than the process actually needs.

What verification does not prove

A successful online check does not prove that the person handing you the document is the named clearance holder. If identity matters for your process, combine verification with an appropriate identity check, such as comparing the presenter against a government-issued photo ID. It also does not decide suitability: employment, licensing, visa and legal decisions remain with the receiving organization, not with the verification result.

If the verification shows no record or does not match

Do not immediately label a clearance fake. A failed check can also result from a typing mistake, a character being read incorrectly, a temporary portal issue or a record that needs official help.

Try these steps in order

  1. Confirm that you are on clearance.nbi.gov.ph.
  2. Re-read every requested character from the original document or a clear copy.
  3. Check for confusing characters, extra spaces or information entered into the wrong field.
  4. Retry later if the official portal is unavailable or does not complete the check.
  5. If the result still fails or the details differ, ask the clearance holder to contact the NBI and resolve the discrepancy through official channels.

Confusing characters and poor copies

Most failed checks we see described come down to reading the document wrong. The characters 0 and O, 1, I and lowercase l, and 5 and S are easy to confuse on a printed clearance, especially on a photocopy or a photo of a photo. Work from the clearest version of the document you have, enter one field at a time, and double-check anything you typed from memory.

When to contact the NBI

If a careful re-check still shows no record, or the official result and the document disagree on material details, the issue needs official help — not another unofficial attempt. The NBI publishes separate contact details for clearance inquiries and its Clearance Center. Use the current information on the official NBI contact page instead of numbers copied from social-media comments. Our Help Center also collects troubleshooting steps for common portal problems.

Do not “fix” a mismatch yourself. Editing a PDF, image, printed name, date or verification screenshot can make the document unreliable. Corrections must be handled through the NBI.

Warning signs that need official confirmation

  • The online result and the document show different names or other material details.
  • The document has visible erasures, pasted text, inconsistent type or altered dates.
  • A QR code or link sends you to a non-government domain or requests a download.
  • The holder supplies only a cropped verification screenshot and will not allow a fresh official check.
  • The clearance is unreadable, incomplete or outside the validity accepted by the requesting organization.

None of these signals alone should replace official confirmation. When the result matters, resolve the issue with the NBI rather than relying on a third-party verifier or social-media answer.

What to do when you spot a warning sign

Run a fresh verification yourself on the official portal instead of relying on a screenshot supplied by the holder. A cropped or edited screenshot proves nothing — the live portal result is the signal that counts. If the fresh check disagrees with the document, stop the process and escalate the mismatch to the NBI before making any decision based on the clearance.

Why unofficial verifiers are not a substitute

Third-party “verification” websites and social-media helpers have no access to NBI records, so their answers cannot confirm anything — and the forms they provide may exist mainly to collect personal data. Check the latest updates page for any current notices about the official portal before trusting an unfamiliar service.

Protect the clearance holder’s personal data

NBI Clearance is an identity-related document. Do not publish an unredacted copy in a Facebook group, forum, chat room or public support ticket. Do not send the full clearance number, QR code, fingerprint image, birth details, address or ID copy to an unofficial “agent.”

If you are an employer or other receiving organization, explain why the document is needed, restrict access, keep it only as long as required and use a secure channel. For a simple authenticity check, go to the official portal instead of uploading the document to a third-party website.

If you are the clearance holder

Keep the original in a safe place and share copies sparingly. If an employer or agency legitimately needs a copy, ask why, send it through a secure channel, and redact details that are not necessary for the check — a full unredacted scan sitting in an email thread or chat group is a common way personal data leaks.

If you are an employer or HR staff

Tell the applicant exactly why you need the clearance and what you will do with it. Limit access to the staff who actually run the check, store any copy securely, and delete it once the hiring or enrollment decision no longer requires it. When a simple yes-or-no on authenticity is all you need, the official portal check replaces the need to keep a copy at all.

NBI Clearance verification FAQs

Where can I verify an NBI Clearance online?

Use the NBI Clearance Online Verification option on the official portal at clearance.nbi.gov.ph. Avoid unofficial forms that ask you to submit clearance or identity information.

Can an employer verify an NBI Clearance?

The official portal describes the service as a way to check authenticity and validity. Employers should compare the official result with the document presented and handle applicant data lawfully and securely.

Why does NBI online verification show no record?

Possible reasons include an input mistake, an unclear character, a temporary portal issue or a record that needs official support. Recheck the document and contact the NBI if the issue remains unresolved.

Does a successful online check replace inspecting the document?

No. Compare the official result with the presented clearance, check its stated validity and resolve any material mismatch before relying on it.

Can I verify a clearance through this website?

No. NBI Clearance Help is an independent information site. We do not accept clearance numbers, identity documents or personal data and cannot access NBI records.

Should I post my NBI Clearance online to ask if it is real?

No. Use the official verification service. If legitimate support requires a copy, redact information that is not necessary and send it only through an authorized, secure channel.

What information do I need to enter for online verification?

Enter the details requested by the live verification service, reading them carefully from the clearance being checked. The portal interface can change, so follow the current official prompts rather than an old screenshot or third-party form.

What should I do if the portal result and the document do not match?

Treat a material mismatch as unresolved. Do not edit the document to “fix” it. Ask the clearance holder to resolve the discrepancy through official NBI channels before relying on the document.
