How to Verify an NBI Clearance Online

The official NBI Clearance portal includes an Online Verification service for checking a clearance’s authenticity and validity. Use that official service, compare its result with the document presented, and protect the clearance holder’s personal information.

Open Official NBI Portal ↗ See verification steps

By NBI Clearance Help Editorial Team · Published and reviewed 25 September 2026

Quick answer

Go directly to clearance.nbi.gov.ph and choose NBI Clearance Online Verification under NBI Clearance eServices. The official portal says this service checks a clearance’s authenticity and validity.

Use only the official NBI domain. This independent website does not verify clearances and must not receive a clearance number, QR code, ID image, fingerprints, date of birth, password or OTP.

How to verify NBI Clearance online

  1. Open the official portal yourself. Type clearance.nbi.gov.ph into the browser or use the official link above. Check the domain before entering any document information.
  2. Find NBI Clearance eServices. On the public portal, select NBI Clearance Online Verification.
  3. Use the details requested by the live service. Read them carefully from the clearance being checked. The portal interface can change, so follow the current official prompts rather than an old screenshot or third-party form.
  4. Review the official response. Check whether the service confirms the record and compare the displayed information with the clearance presented to you.
  5. Check the document itself. Confirm that the name and relevant details match, the document is readable and its printed validity or expiration information meets the requesting organization’s rules.
For employers and organizations: verification is one control, not permission to collect or retain more personal information than necessary. Limit access to authorized staff and follow applicable privacy rules.

What online verification can—and cannot—tell you

Suitability decisionA verification result confirms document information; it does not make an employment, licensing, visa or legal decision for the receiving organization.

If the verification shows no record or does not match

Do not immediately label a clearance fake. A failed check can also result from a typing mistake, a character being read incorrectly, a temporary portal issue or a record that needs official help.

Try these steps in order

  1. Confirm that you are on clearance.nbi.gov.ph.
  2. Re-read every requested character from the original document or a clear copy.
  3. Check for confusing characters, extra spaces or information entered into the wrong field.
  4. Retry later if the official portal is unavailable or does not complete the check.
  5. If the result still fails or the details differ, ask the clearance holder to contact the NBI and resolve the discrepancy through official channels.

The NBI publishes separate contact details for clearance inquiries and its Clearance Center. Use the current information on the official NBI contact page instead of numbers copied from social-media comments.

Do not “fix” a mismatch yourself. Editing a PDF, image, printed name, date or verification screenshot can make the document unreliable. Corrections must be handled through the NBI.

Warning signs that need official confirmation

  • The online result and the document show different names or other material details.
  • The document has visible erasures, pasted text, inconsistent type or altered dates.
  • A QR code or link sends you to a non-government domain or requests a download.
  • The holder supplies only a cropped verification screenshot and will not allow a fresh official check.
  • The clearance is unreadable, incomplete or outside the validity accepted by the requesting organization.

None of these signals alone should replace official confirmation. When the result matters, resolve the issue with the NBI rather than relying on a third-party verifier or social-media answer.

Protect the clearance holder’s personal data

NBI Clearance is an identity-related document. Do not publish an unredacted copy in a Facebook group, forum, chat room or public support ticket. Do not send the full clearance number, QR code, fingerprint image, birth details, address or ID copy to an unofficial “agent.”

If you are an employer or other receiving organization, explain why the document is needed, restrict access, keep it only as long as required and use a secure channel. For a simple authenticity check, go to the official portal instead of uploading the document to a third-party website.

NBI Clearance verification FAQs

Where can I verify an NBI Clearance online?

Use the NBI Clearance Online Verification option on the official portal at clearance.nbi.gov.ph. Avoid unofficial forms that ask you to submit clearance or identity information.

Can an employer verify an NBI Clearance?

The official portal describes the service as a way to check authenticity and validity. Employers should compare the official result with the document presented and handle applicant data lawfully and securely.

Why does the verification show no record?

Possible reasons include an input mistake, an unclear character, a temporary portal issue or a record that needs official support. Recheck the document and contact the NBI if the issue remains unresolved.

Does a successful check replace inspecting the document?

No. Compare the official result with the presented clearance, check its stated validity and resolve any material mismatch before relying on it.

Can I verify a clearance through this website?

No. NBI Clearance Help is an independent information site. We do not accept clearance numbers, identity documents or personal data and cannot access NBI records.

Should I post my clearance online to ask whether it is real?

No. Use the official verification service. If legitimate support requires a copy, redact information that is not necessary and send it only through an authorized, secure channel.
