Editorial Policy

How NBI Clearance Help researches, verifies, updates and labels official facts, portal observations and source conflicts.

Official NBI Portal ↗

Our editorial goal is to publish practical, accurate guidance that serves NBI Clearance applicants without presenting an independent website as a government service.

People-first scope

Every guide must address a defined applicant need, such as preparing documents, understanding a payment status or choosing the correct renewal route. We do not create near-duplicate location or keyword pages merely to attract search visits. There is no target word count; a page should be as complete as the user’s task requires and no longer than necessary.

Primary sources come first

For procedures, eligibility, fees, identity documents, processing information, offices and digital services, we prioritize NBI.gov.ph, clearance.nbi.gov.ph, official Citizen’s Charter material and other relevant Philippine government publications. Third-party sources may help us identify a question, but they are not treated as authority for an official requirement.

How we verify a claim

  1. Find the page or document issued by the responsible government agency.
  2. Check whether the information applies to the standard process or a special route.
  3. Compare related official pages for differences in dates, amounts or instructions.
  4. State important limitations and link readers to the live official transaction system.
  5. Review other pages on this site that may repeat the same fact.

Conflicting or incomplete official information

Government pages are not always updated at the same time. If current official sources disagree, we identify the conflict, explain which source appears newer or closer to the live transaction and avoid claiming certainty that the evidence does not support. The amount, appointment option or instruction generated by the official system controls the user’s actual transaction.

Portal observations and illustrations

A live portal can change without notice. Interface observations are described as observations, not permanent policy. Original diagrams are labeled as explanatory illustrations and are not presented as official screenshots.

Authorship, review and automation

NBI Clearance Help is responsible for the final published text. Research and drafting tools may assist with organization, but factual claims are checked against cited primary sources before publication. Automated output is not treated as evidence. Pages are reviewed for clarity, internal consistency, misleading wording and unsafe requests for personal information.

Updates and dates

We update a page when its substance changes or when a new official source materially affects the guidance. We do not change dates simply to make old content appear fresh. Topics that change frequently, including fees, branch availability and portal features, require more frequent checking than stable background explanations.

Corrections and reader feedback

Material errors are corrected promptly and related pages are checked for the same statement. Readers can send the page URL, disputed sentence and supporting official source to helpnbiclearance@gmail.com or use our contact form.
