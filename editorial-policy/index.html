Editorial Policy

How NBI Clearance Help researches, verifies, updates and labels official facts, portal observations and source conflicts.

Official NBI Portal ↗

Our goal is to publish clear, useful information without pretending that a third-party guide is an official government source.

Primary-source first

For process, fees, requirements, offices, eligibility and digital services, we prioritize NBI.gov.ph, clearance.nbi.gov.ph, official NBI Citizen’s Charter pages and official government announcements.

Conflicting official sources

Government pages are not always updated at the same time. For example, the current NBI how-to page lists a ₱130 basic fee plus an e-payment service charge, while an older Citizen’s Charter page still displays legacy figures. When we find a conflict, we disclose it and point users to the live official system for the final transaction.

Last-verified dates

Important pages display a verification date. High-change topics such as fees, eGovPH and branches should be checked more often than stable background explanations.

Corrections

When we discover a material error, we update the relevant page and review linked pages that may repeat the same claim.

Independent, source-checked NBI Clearance guidance for people in the Philippines. We do not issue clearances, process NBI payments, or represent the National Bureau of Investigation.
